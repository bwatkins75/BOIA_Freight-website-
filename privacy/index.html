Privacy notice

Last updated November 4, 2026

This notice covers boiafreight.com, a marketing site for BOIA Freight. The site has no accounts, no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies.

What we collect

Only what you type into the demo request form: your name, company, fleet size, the best time to reach you, your work email and, if you add it, a phone number. Along with the request we log the time it was sent and the sending IP address, which we use only to block spam and abuse.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the demo you asked for.
  • To keep spam and automated abuse off the form.

We don't sell your information, rent it, or use it for advertising, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Who processes it

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the site and delivers the form to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile runs the spam check on the form. It looks at signals from your browser to tell people from bots, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typeface, so your browser requests the font files from Google.

How long we keep it

Demo requests stay in our inbox for as long as we're in touch about a demo, and no longer than 24 months after the last contact. Spam-control records (IP address and time) are deleted automatically within a few hours.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what you sent. Send a request through the demo request form and say what you'd like done; we'll confirm by replying to the email you gave us.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date above.
